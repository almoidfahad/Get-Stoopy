# Checkout pricing arithmetic foundation (not a quote)

At this increment customer delivery pricing was undecided. The owner has since supplied tier amounts for Mymensingh City and Purbadhala Sadar; classification, multi-store treatment and activation remain unresolved (see `docs/181-cod-delivery-areas-and-slab-amounts.md`). Rider earnings are a separate, previously approved development policy and must never be used as the customer fee. This increment introduces an **unwired arithmetic-only** module, `packages/core/src/checkout-pricing.ts`; it defines no rate, jurisdiction, routing, tax, discount, campaign, allocation or activation policy. It is not exported from the core package barrel or used by the API/UI and cannot enable an order, COD or payment. All examples/tests use synthetic numbers, not approved fees.

The calculator accepts an explicit product subtotal, already-allocated product discount, customer delivery fee and already-calculated tax for each group, all as nonnegative `bigint` minor units and with matching three-uppercase-letter currency codes. It does not accept missing values as zero. Each group requires a unique stable ID, a positive subtotal and a product discount no greater than that subtotal. It calculates `subtotal − product discount + delivery fee + tax` per group, then sums each component and total, checking PostgreSQL signed-BIGINT limits at every step. Output groups are copied and sorted by ID for deterministic presentation. It does not compute or allocate discounts, taxes or delivery fees, validate ISO currency membership, determine whether a free total is allowed, or decide whether tax is included/excluded; those are policy responsibilities.

Unit tests cover multi-store reconciliation, deterministic ordering, explicit zero, missing/invalid values, duplicates, mixed currencies, over-discount, exact signed-BIGINT boundary and overflow, and group/parent consistency over 1–50 groups. This is not an authoritative financial snapshot: neither provenance nor cart lines, applicable policy version, seller, destination, time, promotion funding, jurisdiction or tax allocation is validated by this function. Before connecting anything to quote or payment, obtain owner-approved customer pricing and tax/discount decisions; derive every input server-side against a verified recipient destination and cart, define and persist immutable policy/source snapshots and group allocations, add DB constraints and transactional concurrency/replay guarantees, and independently verify cancellation, refunds, COD and accounting. Other charges require an explicit policy and contract rather than silently omitting them. No database schema, hosted data, feature flag, live fee, quote API or deployment changed.
