# Fail closed on overlapping delivery-preview zones

The geographic design requires ambiguity to be rejected until an explicit overlapping-zone priority policy is approved. The delivery preview already rejected multiple approved effective boundaries for the selected zone, but a caller could choose one of two different active zones that both covered the same destination. That could select an arbitrary city, store coverage or future delivery policy.

The unsaved preview now also rejects a point covered by an approved, effective boundary belonging to a different active zone in an active city within the cart's country. The test-only draft save and saved-draft preview reuse the same validation. It returns the existing generic `CHECKOUT_DESTINATION_NOT_SERVICEABLE` rather than disclosing other zone IDs, and continues not to echo contact or coordinates. `ST_Covers` includes edges, so a shared edge between two zones is deliberately rejected; a single-zone outer edge remains covered. Different countries, inactive zones/cities, unapproved and future boundary versions do not participate in the new check. Store coverage cannot resolve the ambiguity: another overlapping active zone blocks even if not served by the stores.

The query samples database time once and runs within the existing repeatable-read transaction. Disposable integration fixtures exercise non-overlap, shared edge, inactive/future/unapproved competing zones, overlap from another city in the same country, unsaved and saved previews, draft-save rejection, no writes/PII, and an activation interleaving. An in-flight saved preview can still return its earlier consistent snapshot after another zone is activated; later attempts reject. A preview is not a promise or reservation.

There is **still no active-boundary pointer**: any approved effective history for a competing active zone can conservatively block a point even if operations consider that historical boundary superseded. Do not delete approved history to clear a failure. A real activation/review workflow, immutable history, explicit overlap/tie policy, transactional quote-time revalidation, cross-city/country and geometry operational review remain necessary. The dispatch and post-order destination guards still follow older rules and were not changed here. No migration, live database change, production deployment or checkout activation was performed.
